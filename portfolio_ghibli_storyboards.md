# 30-Second Hybrid Ghibli × Pixar Explanatory Video Storyboards (Current Branch Theme Edition)

This production specification defines a uniform **30-second explanatory video storyboard (6 beats × 5.0 seconds)** for all four projects in `zendrix-hub/cowboy-portfolio` (current branch snapshot `296f0352`). It combines the hand-drawn character construction and directional cel shading from [How to Draw Ghibli Style STEP BY STEP by SamDoesArts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1NTi5q5Vh_Q) with the 5-step Pixar-inspired cartoon stylization ("Intangible Vibe," "Rule of Threes" core shapes, "Rule of T" proportions, and 3-step Flat-Shadow-Highlight rendering) from [How to Draw Cartoon People (from Reference) by KeshArt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V-vPud10lAA), engineered to blend directly into the dark zinc (`#09090b`), glassmorphic card spotlight (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.14)`), and 4-stage `ArchitectureFlow.tsx` UI of your current branch.
